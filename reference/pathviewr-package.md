# pathviewr: Wrangle, Analyze, and Visualize Animal Movement Data

Tools to import, clean, and visualize movement data, particularly from
motion capture systems such as Optitrack's 'Motive', the Straw Lab's
'Flydra', or from other sources. We provide functions to remove
artifacts, standardize tunnel position and tunnel axes, select a region
of interest, isolate specific trajectories, fill gaps in trajectory
data, and calculate 3D and per-axis velocity. For experiments of visual
guidance, we also provide functions that use subject position to
estimate perception of visual stimuli.
